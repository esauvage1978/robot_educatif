Before buying, four questions really matter: **Wi-Fi or 4G**, **continuous recording or on detection**, **Battery Capacity** and **Storage**.

## Wi-Fi or 4G: the first criterion

- **Wi-Fi (S7, ZY-C2, ZS-GX4S)** : the camera must capture your box **2.4 GHz** where you fix it. Check the signal with your phone before drilling. No subscription required for live video.
- **4G (ZY-G4)** : the form indicates **"4G LTE only"** : it takes a SIM card and a fixed fee. It is the solution for a plot, a barn, a construction site or a second home **Without the Internet**.

## Continuous recording (AOV) or on detection

- **On detection (ZY-C2, ZS-GX4S)**: the camera sleeps and wakes up when the PIR sensor detects movement (range announced 15 m for the ZY-C2, 10 m for the ZS-GX4S). It is the most battery-efficient mode, but the first seconds of a pass may be missing.
- **Continuous 24/7 "AOV" (S7, ZY-G4)** : the camera is constantly filming in addition to the alerts. These two models logically have a larger battery (**9,000 mAh** against 5,200 mAh) and accept SD cards up to **256 GB**. In winter, with little sun, the autonomy depends a lot on the orientation of the panel.

## Installation: the points to check

1. **Orientation of the solar panel** : due south if possible, without shadow, inclination of about 30 to 45 °; the panel cable makes **3 m** (official fact sheet).
2. **Height**: 2.5 to 3 m, out of reach, aiming at the passage area rather than the horizon.
3. **microSD card**: not always included; respect the maximum capacity indicated (128 GB or 256 GB depending on the model).
4. Panel **temperature range**: -20 °C to 60 °C (official sheet).
5. **Full Charge** the battery before installation.
