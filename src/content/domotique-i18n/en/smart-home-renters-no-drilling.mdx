Being a tenant does not prevent you from having a smart home. The rule is simple: **anything that plugs, sticks or screws on an existing tip** can be installed and left with you. What concerns the electrical panel, the built-in switches or the boiler requires the agreement of the owner and, often, an electrician.

**Our best choice to start renting: a Zigbee dongle on Home Assistant, plus Zigbee sockets and sensors.** Nothing is fixed to the wall, and on the day of the move the whole system fits in a carton. ## Equipment without risk

**Connected sockets.** This is the ideal input: programming of an auxiliary heating, measurement of consumption, remote shutdown. Respect the maximum power indicated by the manufacturer (often 16 A) and avoid multiple outlets cascade. Our [Comparison of Zigbee plugs](/en/smart-home/smart-plugs-uk-us/) Which ones measure consumption.

**Battery-operated sensors.** Temperature, humidity, window opening, water leakage: they land or stick. To choose, see our [Comparison of Zigbee Temperature Sensors](/en/smart-home/zigbee-temperature-sensor/). Use repositionable adhesives to avoid damaging the paint.

**Wireless buttons.** A Zigbee button glued near the door can turn off all the lamps when leaving, without touching the existing wiring.

## Equipment to check

**Thermostatic heads**. On a hot water radiator equipped with a thermostatic valve, the head is unscrewed and replaced by a connected head, without draining. Keep the old head and put it back at the start. Check the compatibility of the thread with the adapter provided.

**Cameras**. An indoor camera does not pose a technical problem. However, filming the common areas of a building or the public road is not allowed: only direct it to your home, as recalled by the [CNIL](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/la-videosurveillance-videoprotection-chez-soi).

## What to leave to the owner

Built-in modules behind switches, roller shutters, electrical panel, boiler thermostat: these installations modify the housing. Ask for written authorization and call an electrician.

## The brain: discreet and transportable

A [Home Assistant Green or a Raspberry Pi 5](/en/smart-home/home-assistant-green-vs-raspberry-pi-5/) is connected to the Internet router via a network cable. At the move, you reconnect it at home: automations and history follow.
