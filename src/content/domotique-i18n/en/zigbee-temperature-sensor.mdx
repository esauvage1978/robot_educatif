- Aqara T1 (CR2032), SNZB-02P (CR2477), Tuya (AAA)

### Criterion 4: Number of sensors required

**Recommendations**:
- **Studio 30 m2**: 1 sensor (living room/single room)

**Rule**: 1 sensor per heated room or room to be monitored. No need in toilet, hallways, dressing room.

## Optimal placement of sensors

### Where to place?

**Recommended**:
- Room center (not against outside wall)
- Height 1.21.8 m from the ground (breathing area)
- Shelf, furniture, office
- In the shade (not direct sunlight)

**To avoid**:
- Close Window (False Air Currents Measure)
- Above radiator (local temperature  piece)
- Behind thick curtain (stagnant air)
- Kitchen near plate (steam, heat)
- Bathroom (humidity > 80%  sensor condensation)

### Distance from Zigbee dongle

**Direct range**: 1015 m indoor (23 walls)

**If > 15 m** : add Zigbee repeaters (plugs) between dongle and sensor. [Zigbee Takes Guide](/en/smart-home/smart-plugs-uk-us/).

## Installation and configuration

### Pairing with ZHA

1. Open Home Assistant  **Zigbee Devices (ZHA)  Add**
2. Remove tab battery SNZB-02D
3. Maintain **Button** Sensor **5 seconds**
4. LED flashes (red/blue)
5. Sensor appears: "SONOFF SNZB-02D"

**Entities created**:
- `sensor.snzb_02d_salon_temperature` (°C)
- `sensor.snzb_02d_salon_humidity` (%)
- `sensor.snzb_02d_salon_battery` (%)

### Calibration (optional)

If deviation (1 °C vs. reference thermometer):

```yaml
# configuration.yaml
sensor:
  - platform: template
    sensors:
      temperature_salon_calibree:
        friendly_name: "Température salon (calibrée)"
        unit_of_measurement: "°C"
        value_template: "{{ states('sensor.snzb_02d_salon_temperature')|float - 0.5 }}"
```

Adjust `-0,5` according to measured deviation.

## Common Errors and Troubleshooting

### Problem: Sensor does not pair

**Solutions**:
1. Remove tab stack completely (check contact stack)
2. Move closer to the Zigbee dongle ( 3 m)
3. Reset: hold button 10 seconds (orange LED  reset)
4. New battery (some new batteries are low after 1 year of storage)

### Problem: Incorrect temperature (deviation > 2 °C)

**Causes**:
- Sensor in full sun (local temperature  ambient)
- Too close to radiator or window
- Defective sensor

**Solutions**:
1. Move sensor (room center, shadow)
2. Wait for stabilization (30 min after travel)
3. Compare with medical thermometer (reliable reference)
4. Calibrate via template sensor (see Calibration section)

### Problem: Low battery after 6 months

**Causes**:
- Low-end battery (actual capacity  announced)
- Defective sensor (current leakage)
- Old firmware (excessive consumption)

**Solutions**:
1. Replace with battery **Duracell or Energizer** (duration 2 no-name batteries)
2. Check firmware sensor (ZHA  Device  Firmware OTA)
3. If recurring: defective sensor, after-sales return

### Problem: Humidity blocked at 99%

**Cause**: condensation on sensor (bathroom, cellar very humid).

**Solutions**:
1. Remove battery, allow to dry 24h (rice or dehumidifier)
2. Avoid parts > 80% humidity (sensor limits)
3. Use waterproof IP65 sensor (more expensive, rare in Zigbee)

### Ideal Humidity

**WHO Recommendations**:
- **4060% RH**: optimal comfort zone
- 30%: too dry air (irritation, static electricity)
- > 70%: risk of mold, dust mites

**Actions according to humidity**:
- 30%: humidifier or water bowls on radiator
- > 70%: ventilate 10 min 2/day, dehumidifier if recurrent

## FAQ

**How accurate is it to automate heating?**
0.5 °C is enough. Same sensor Tuya 0.5 °C allows to "light radiator if 19 °C" without problem. Accuracy 0.2 °C (SNZB-02D) is a comfort bonus.

**How many sensors for a 100 m2 house?**
45 sensors: living room, 23 bedrooms, office or cellar. No need in toilet, hallways, dressing room (identical temperature adjacent rooms).

**Is temperature sensor enough for VMC?**
No, VMC requires sensor **CO2** (air quality). Temperature + humidity detect overheating/humidity, but not air pollution. [CO2 sensor project ESP32](/en/smart-home/esp32-smart-home-projects/).

**Difference Between Zigbee and ESP32 DIY Sensor?**
**Zigbee** : battery, no cable, plug & play, 12 year battery life. **ESP32** : 5V USB power supply, customizable, often cheaper (an ESP32 and a DHT22 are enough). [Tuto ESP32 Temperature](/en/smart-home/esp32-esphome-temperature-sensor/).

**Can I use the sensor outside?**
No (IP20 only). For outdoor use: waterproof IP65 sensor (Aqara IP55, rare) or ESP32 + DHT22 in DIY waterproof housing.

**Do we need to calibrate all the sensors?**
Not if spread < 1 °C. Calibrer uniquement si écart > 1.5 °C vs medical thermometer reference.
