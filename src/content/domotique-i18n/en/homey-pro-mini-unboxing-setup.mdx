*Review started on 23 October 2026.*

The Homey Pro mini arrived this morning. It's the compact version of Athom's Homey Pro, a small hub that acts as the "brain" of a smart home: it speaks Zigbee 3.0, Thread and Matter out of the box and connects to your router with an Ethernet cable. For Z-Wave, 433 MHz, infrared and Bluetooth, you add the Homey Bridge, a small radio box sold separately. Before we hand it any plugs, bulbs or sensors, here's step one: what's in the box and how the setup goes, from the shipping carton to the "All set!" screen.

## At a glance

**What we liked so far**
- A **very easy, well-guided setup**: about 15 minutes from plugging in to the final screen.
- The software update starts by itself on first boot (4 minutes for us).
- A **built-in RJ45 Ethernet port**, with a network cable in the box.
- You set up your home (floors, rooms) right from the start, so there's no tidying up to do later.
- Plenty of technologies on offer when adding a device: 433 MHz, infrared, Matter, Z-Wave, Zigbee. Note that on the mini, 433 MHz, infrared and Z-Wave only work with the Homey Bridge, sold separately.

**Drawbacks so far**
- The Ethernet adapter we got alongside it is no use on the mini, which already has its own network port.
- We haven't paired any devices yet, so radio range, responsiveness and automations are still to be judged. No rating for now; it will come with the next part of the review.

1. the Homey Pro mini hub;
2. a flat Ethernet cable;
3. a USB-C cable;
4. a USB mains charger;

## Ports and the Ethernet adapter

Round the back there are just two ports: an **RJ45 Ethernet** port and a **USB-C** port for power.

<Photo portrait src="/images/domotique/homey/homey-pro-mini-connectique.webp" alt="Back of the Homey Pro mini with the RJ45 Ethernet port and the USB-C port" caption="Ethernet and USB-C ports on the back." />

Along with the mini we'd received the "Homey Pro Ethernet Adapter", a USB-C to Ethernet adapter. It's meant for the full-size Homey Pro, which has no network port. **On the mini it's pointless**: you simply plug in the Ethernet cable that comes in the box. So there's no need to add it to your basket if you're going for the mini. The full-size review is [Homey Pro: unboxing and setup](/en/smart-home/homey-pro-unboxing-setup/).

<Photo portrait src="/images/domotique/homey/homey-pro-mini-adaptateur.webp" alt="Homey Pro Ethernet Adapter, a grey unit with a USB-C cable" caption="The Ethernet adapter, only useful for the full-size Homey Pro." />

## Plugging it in

We connected the Homey Pro mini by Ethernet to a Netgear GS108 switch, then plugged in the USB-C power. The LED lights up purple: the hub is ready to be set up. The cable is required: the mini has no built-in Wi-Fi. It still controls your Wi-Fi devices through your router.

## Setting up in the app, step by step

You download the Homey app by scanning the QR code on the leaflet. Here's how it went for us:

1. **Welcome**: the app shows a welcome screen and offers to set up a Homey.
2. **Creating** your Homey **account**.
3. **Software update**, started automatically. For us it took about 4 minutes (from 12:12 to 12:16). Don't unplug the hub while it runs.
4. **"Welcome home"**: the home setup begins.
5. **Number of floors**: we entered two.
6. **Choosing the floors**: ground floor and first floor in our case. The app also offers basement, attic, garage, pool, garden, terrace and more.
7. **Rooms on each floor**: living room, kitchen, bathroom, bedroom, kids' room, office, and so on.
8. **Summary** of the home as a tree view.
9. **Thread network**: the app asks for permission to set it up.
10. **"All set!"**, at around 12:25.
11. **New device**: the add-device screen offers 433 MHz, Infrared, Matter, Z-Wave and Zigbee. On the mini, only Matter and Zigbee work without an accessory; the others need the Homey Bridge.

## FAQ

**Do you need the Ethernet adapter for the Homey Pro mini?**
No. The mini already has an RJ45 port and a flat Ethernet cable is included. The adapter is for the full-size Homey Pro.

**How long does the setup take?**
About 15 minutes for us, including 4 minutes of automatic software update.

**How do you download the Homey app?**
With the QR code on the leaflet, which takes you to setup.homey.app.

**Which technologies show up when you add a device?**
433 MHz, infrared, Matter, Z-Wave and Zigbee. A Thread network is set up during installation. On the mini, Zigbee, Thread and Matter are built in; Z-Wave, 433 MHz, infrared and Bluetooth need the Homey Bridge, sold separately.

**Which protocol is best to start with?**
It depends on the devices you have in mind: our guide [Zigbee, Wi-Fi, Thread or Matter](/en/smart-home/smart-home-protocols-zigbee-wifi-thread-matter/) explains the differences.
