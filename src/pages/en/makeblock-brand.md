<h2 id="strengths">Strengths (from our pages)</h2>
<ul>
<li>Complete robot: motors, ultrasonic sensor, line follower, lights and buzzer.</li>
<li>mBlock, close to Scratch, with plenty of tutorials.</li>
<li>Good value against pricier “school” kits; repairable and expandable.</li>
<li>A path from blocks to Python on the mBot2 (CyberPi) without changing brand.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="limits">Limits (from our pages)</h2>
<ul>
<li>Screws and cables: it is not snap-together like LEGO.</li>
<li>First Bluetooth and driver setup can take 30–90 minutes on some PCs.</li>
<li>Younger children (6–8) usually need an adult for updates and pairing.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="warranty">Warranty and after-sales service</h2>
<p>Makeblock’s official warranty (checked 23 October 2026): 12 months on electronic modules, Codey Rocky and CyberPi, 6 months on batteries, 3 months on motors; cables, mechanical parts and consumables are not covered unless received damaged. Support starts remotely (service@makeblock.com or chat) and may involve a local repair centre. Your local consumer rights still apply (in the UK, the Consumer Rights Act; check with the retailer first). Source: <a href="https://www.makeblock.com/pages/makeblock-warranty" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Makeblock warranty</a>.</p>
<h2 id="verdict">Our verdict</h2>
<aside class="article-callout" role="note">
<p><strong>In short</strong></p>
<p>Makeblock is our default pick for a first serious programmable robot from around age 8, with some adult help at the start: mBot for a tight budget, mBot2 for a longer project with Python.</p>
