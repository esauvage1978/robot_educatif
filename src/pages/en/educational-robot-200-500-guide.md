## How to choose

From 8 to 10, visual blocks and a robot that answers quickly (light, sound, movement) matter more than a long manual. From 10 to 14, the child can hold a project across several sessions: conditions, several sensors, a build that stays together. From 14, Arduino, a little C, or Python on some platforms, and car-plus-modules kits, fit a motivated secondary student.

| Type | What the child actually does |
|------|------------------------------|
| **Mobile robot** | A course, an obstacle, a result in the room |
| **Construction kit** | Mechanics, patience, several models from the same parts |
| **Interactive robot** | Voice, face, dialogue, rather than a race |

A first discovery should succeed with little friction. Structured learning needs worksheets and a path. A hobby can live with dense documentation and less guidance day to day.

## Just under £200: mBot Ranger

### Ohbot v2.1

![Ohbot educational robot head](/images/blog/robots-educatif-200-euros-2026/ohbot-robot-educatif.jpg)
