**As of 28 October 2026, Makeblock has not announced any mBot3 with a confirmed release date.**

Teacher forums and a few resellers mention an « mBot3 » for **2027**, with no Makeblock product sheet or dedicated mBlock device profile. Makeblock's public roadmap instead pushes **mBot2** and the **mBot2 Rover** add-on (off-road chassis for an existing CyberPi).

## Why Rover fills the mBot3 gap

- **CyberPi** stays the brain—no need to replace the whole fleet.
- Extra **mBuild** modules beat waiting for a new generation.
- **Cost**: Rover + mBot2 often undercuts a hypothetical premium mBot3.
