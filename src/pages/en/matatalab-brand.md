> **Quick answer (November 2026)** — The **Tale-Bot Pro** is an excellent **screen-free** first robot for **ages 3 to 5**: children use story and counting modes alone for long sessions. Handle costumes gently; check the Android app availability in your region.

Matatalab (official store now trading as **MatataStudio**) makes screen-free coding robots for young children. We tested its **Tale-Bot Pro** at home for several days with our **4-year-old** son. This page summarises that test in English; the day-one diary is still available in French.

## Our hands-on test (summary)

We unboxed two boxes: the robot (already charged), interchangeable arms (wings, Lego-compatible mounts, marker holders), three costumes (rabbit, police, boy), a language/distance config board (10 cm or 15 cm steps), interactive story cards, and manuals.

**Story mode:** after switching the bot to French, our son listened to the zoo story **six times in one hour** — clear voice, simple buttons. At **3 years 10 months** he was fully independent on this activity.

**Counting and shapes:** the “find six strawberries” game worked well once he learned to press **Play** to validate an answer (small UX detail for toddlers).

**Costumes:** he loved swapping outfits alone; plastic accessories **started to wear** after repeated changes — our main durability concern.

Full French write-up: [Tale-Bot Pro unboxing and first steps](/deballage-et-premier-pas-du-robot-tale-bot-de-chez-matatalab/) · overview: [Tale-Bot educational robot](/le-robot-tale-bot-de-chez-matatalab-un-outil-educatif-pour-enfants/).

## Strengths (our test)

- Clear, audible voice.  
- Many activities: stories, counting, shapes, recorded sounds.  
- Suitable from age 3: story mode usable without an adult.  
- Simple and intuitive arrow buttons.  
- Arrives charged and ready to use.

## Limits (our test)

- Fragile costumes and small plastic parts.  
- The Android app was **not on the Play Store** when we tested (check current MatataStudio listings).

## Warranty and after-sales service

MatataStudio official store (checked 23 October 2026): 1-year limited warranty from receipt for manufacturing defects; repair or replacement parts, with one-way shipping covered for eligible claims; 30-day returns on official-store orders.
