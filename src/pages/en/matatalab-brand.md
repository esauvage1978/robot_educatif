<p>Matatalab (official store now trading as MatataStudio) makes screen-free coding robots for young children. We tested its <strong>Tale-Bot Pro</strong> at home for several days with our 4-year-old son. Our full test is in French.</p>
<h2 id="our-test">Our test</h2>
<ul>
<li><a hreflang="fr" href="/deballage-et-premier-pas-du-robot-tale-bot-de-chez-matatalab/">Tale-Bot Pro: unboxing and first steps (in French)</a>: story mode, counting, shapes, sound recording, costumes.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="strengths">Strengths (our test)</h2>
<ul>
<li>Clear, audible voice.</li>
<li>Many activities: stories, counting, shapes, recorded sounds.</li>
<li>Suitable from age 3: at 3 years 10 months our son used story mode on his own.</li>
<li>Simple and intuitive.</li>
<li>Arrives charged and ready to use.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="limits">Limits (our test)</h2>
<ul>
<li>Fragile costumes.</li>
<li>The Android app was not on the Play Store when we tested.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="warranty">Warranty and after-sales service</h2>
<p>MatataStudio official store (checked 23 October 2026): 1-year limited warranty from receipt for manufacturing defects; repair or replacement parts, with one-way shipping covered for eligible claims; 30-day returns on official-store orders. Contact support@matatalab.com with your order number. Local consumer rights still apply. Source: <a href="https://shop.matatastudio.com/pages/warranty-policy" target="_blank" rel="noopener">MatataStudio warranty</a>.</p>
<h2 id="verdict">Our verdict</h2>
<aside class="article-callout" role="note">
<p><strong>In short</strong></p>
<p>The Tale-Bot Pro is an excellent first robot for ages 3 to 5: children use it alone, for a long time. Handle the costumes with care.</p>
</aside>
