The Tale-Bot Pro from [Matatalab](/en/matatalab-brand/) is a hands-on educational tool. Children use it to meet robotics and programming. This page answers the questions that come up often about the Tale-Bot.

Searches such as **"robot tale bot"** (with or without a hyphen, or "Tale Bot") usually mean **this** screen-free programmable robot, not a Makeblock robot such as the **mBot**.

You will find a description of the Tale-Bot in [this article](/en/matatalab-tale-bot-educational-robot/), and more practical notes in [this one](/deballage-et-premier-pas-du-robot-tale-bot-de-chez-matatalab/).

## How do you change the Tale-Bot's language?

The Tale-Bot comes with a double-sided board so you can choose among 11 languages.

Language-choice board

To change the language:

* Turn the Tale-Bot on.
* Place it on the flag of the language you want.
* The robot then says **hello** in the language you selected.
* That is the whole procedure.

The setting stays even when the robot is switched off.

## How do you update the Tale-Bot firmware?

Before you start the procedure, always check that the robot is fully charged.

As of 1 May 2023, the procedure does not work on Android. I am in contact with the company to look for a solution.
