Install [mBlock 5 on Windows](/en/install-mblock-5-windows/) first, then look at the [interface](/en/first-steps-mblock-5/). The pictures on this page are the French screens of mBlock 5. The labels you see follow the language you chose in the app.

An account is what Makeblock uses to store projects in their cloud, when that option is on the screen, and to open the same work on another computer. Without an account you can often keep working on the machine, and you keep the files only if you export them yourself.

## 1. Choose the language

mBlock often opens in English. Set the language before you follow the screens.

1. Open the language control in the top bar. It is often a globe or an “A”. The exact spot depends on the version.
2. Choose your language and confirm if the app asks.
3. Restart mBlock if it says the change needs a new launch.

![mBlock 5, French interface: the language list](/images/blog/sinscrire-mblock-compte/01-langue-francais.png)

## 2. Where the account is created

In the installed app, use the windows in the next sections. In a browser, the older community address was [planet.mblock.cc](https://planet.mblock.cc/). Check [Makeblock](https://www.makeblock.com/) if that address has moved.

## 3. Open the account menu

On the right of the top bar, click the profile icon.

![mBlock 5, French interface: the profile icon](/images/blog/sinscrire-mblock-compte/02-menu-compte.png)

## 4. Enter the email

Type an inbox you actually read, then the button that continues (Suivant on the French screen).

![mBlock 5, French interface: the email step](/images/blog/sinscrire-mblock-compte/03-formulaire-email.png)

## 5. Age

Makeblock asks whether you are 16 or older. That step chooses the notices about children and parents.

![mBlock 5, French interface: the 16-or-older question](/images/blog/sinscrire-mblock-compte/04-age-16-ans.png)

If the answer is no, further notices can appear.

![mBlock 5, French interface: the under-16 notice](/images/blog/sinscrire-mblock-compte/05-moins-de-16-ans.png)

![mBlock 5, French interface: the note for a parent](/images/blog/sinscrire-mblock-compte/06-mention-parents.png)

A parent or a teacher can create the account when the school or the family prefers that. Follow the text on the screen and the rules that apply where you live.

## 6. Password

Choose a long password, then the button that creates the account.

![mBlock 5, French interface: password and create account](/images/blog/sinscrire-mblock-compte/07-mot-de-passe-creer-compte.png)

## 7. Confirm the email

Open the message from Makeblock, including the spam folder. Until that link is used, some account features can stay limited.

## 8. The order

| Step | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Set the language |
| 2 | Profile icon, top right |
| 3 | Email, then continue |
| 4 | Say whether you are 16 or older |
| 5 | Password, then create the account |
| 6 | Confirm the email |
