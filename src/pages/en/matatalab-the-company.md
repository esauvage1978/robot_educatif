**[Matatalab](/en/matatalab-brand/)** designs and produces **educational toys for children**. Founded in 2017, the company quickly found an audience outside its home country, thanks to the way it teaches science, technology, engineering and mathematics (STEM).

Matatalab's idea is to offer **educational tools** that invite **creativity**, **curiosity** and **exploration**. The range includes programmable robots, construction kits and learning games, so children meet STEM ideas through play, with their hands on the pieces.

I was lucky enough to receive one of their products, and I put it straight into the hands of my 4-year-old son. His verdict was immediate: it is his favorite toy, and he takes it everywhere.
