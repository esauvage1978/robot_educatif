**Mobsya** shipped **Thymio 3** in **September 2026** at a **€237** list price (EU). The Swiss robot keeps Thymio's hallmarks—many sensors, **VPL** and **Aseba**—with better battery life and a tougher shell for middle school. In the same classroom slot sits **[Makeblock mBot2](/en/makeblock-brand/)** (~€168), covered in our [mBot vs mBot2](/en/mbot-vs-mbot2/) guide.

## What actually changed on Thymio 3

- **Sensors**: horizontal proximity, ground, accelerometer, microphone—about **39 sensor behaviors** in VPL.
- **Runtime**: USB-C Li-ion pack (manufacturer cites **2–3 h** class sessions).
- **Coding**: **VPL3** on the robot (no kid screen required), export to **Aseba Studio**; no native Python like CyberPi.
- **Ecosystem**: Thymio II activities carry over; no Makeblock **mBuild** rails.
