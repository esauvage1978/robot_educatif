## Our best choice to get started

**Home Assistant on a [Home Assistant Green or a Raspberry Pi 5](/en/smart-home/home-assistant-green-vs-raspberry-pi-5/), with a [Zigbee dongle](/en/smart-home/best-zigbee-dongle-home-assistant/).** This is the base we use in all our tutorials: everything stays local, without a subscription, and the system grows with your needs.
