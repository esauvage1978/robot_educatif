## Wi-Fi vs 4G

- **Wi-Fi (S7, ZY-C2, ZS-GX4S):** needs solid **2.4 GHz** at the mount—test with your phone before drilling. No data plan for live view on LAN/WAN.  
- **4G (ZY-G4):** **SIM + data plan** for sites with no broadband—outbuildings, plots, caravans.

## Continuous (AOV) vs motion

- **Motion (ZY-C2, ZS-GX4S):** PIR wake—best battery life; first seconds of an event may be clipped.  
- **24/7 AOV (S7, ZY-G4):** always recording—larger **9000 mAh** packs and up to **256 GB** SD; winter sun and panel aim matter more.

## Install checklist

1. Panel: south-facing (northern hemisphere), ~30–45° tilt, minimal shade—**3 m** panel cable on datasheet.  
2. Height **2.5–3 m**, lens on access paths not the horizon.  
3. microSD often **not included**—respect 128/256 GB limits.  
4. Panel operating range about **−20 °C to 60 °C** (spec).  
5. Fully charge battery before mount.

## UK / US / Canada privacy

Film **your property** only. Avoid public pavement or neighbours’ gardens in frame—use app **privacy/motion zones** if the view includes the street.

- UK: [ICO home CCTV guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-the-public/cctv/)  
- US: state and local surveillance laws vary—check before recording beyond your lot line.  
- Canada: follow provincial privacy guidance for cameras facing public areas.
- France (readers of our French guide): [CNIL — home video surveillance](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/la-videosurveillance-videoprotection-chez-soi/)

## FAQ

- **Cloud required?** All models list optional cloud; **local SD** recording depends on app settings — check the manual for your region.
- **Winter sun?** **Motion-only** models (ZY-C2, ZS-GX4S) last longer on battery; **24/7 AOV** models (S7, ZY-G4) need good panel placement and larger packs (**9000 mAh** on datasheet).
- **What is AOV?** **Always-on video** — the camera records continuously (not only on PIR), which drains battery faster but avoids missing the first seconds of an event.
- **4G data costs?** **ZY-G4** needs a **SIM** and mobile data plan — budget monthly fees like a phone line, not just hardware.
