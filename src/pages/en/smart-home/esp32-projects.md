The ESP32 is a small, inexpensive electronic board with built-in Wi-Fi. With ESPHome, software that prepares its program from a simple settings file, you do not have to write any code, and the board plugs straight into Home Assistant, the free software that gathers your connected devices. Here are our guides, from choosing a board to a complete project.

## Our top pick

**A classic ESP32 board (DevKitC) to start with**, the best documented in ESPHome. Move to the ESP32-S3 for a camera, or to the ESP32-C6 for Thread: see [which ESP32 board to choose](/en/smart-home/which-esp32-board-for-smart-home/).
