On **17 June 2026** the CSA released **Matter 1.6**: NFC commissioning, multi-admin, and new device classes. This matters if you target a [fully local smart home](/domotique/debuter-en-domotique-guide-complet/) without mandatory cloud accounts.

## Highlights

- **NFC pairing** speeds up commissioning versus QR-only flows.
- **Multi-admin** lets Home Assistant, Apple Home and Google Home share one Matter device.
- **Energy & cameras** clusters mature — verify SKU certification before checkout.

## Home Assistant

Install **2026.10** (7 Oct 2026) for Matter fixes. Keep Zigbee sensors on a USB coordinator; see our [100 project ideas](/domotique/configurateur-projet-domotique/) for automation templates.
