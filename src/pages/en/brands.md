<h2 id="how-we-review">How we review a brand</h2>
<p>We list real products we mention in comparisons, cite official specs, and say when we have hands-on time (for example Tale-Bot Pro with a 4-year-old). We do not copy marketing slogans without checking them against classroom use.</p>
