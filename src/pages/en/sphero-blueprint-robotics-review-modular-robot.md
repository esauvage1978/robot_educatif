**Sphero** unveiled **Blueprint Robotics** at trade shows in **September–October 2026**: a **modular** rail/motor/sensor platform for **high school** and club teams, with **Python** and NGSS-aligned lessons.

## Announced strengths

- **Modularity**: reconfigure as cart, arm, or mobile base without soldering.
- **Sensors**: distance, color, IMU—maker-like but pre-wired.
- **Software**: Sphero IDE plus Python export; not mBlock.

## Blueprint vs mBot2 (~€168)

**mBot2** is still faster to deploy in class (mBlock, [comparison](/en/mbot-vs-mbot2/)). **Blueprint** targets **longer** capstone projects after students outgrow **BOLT/Mini** spheres.
