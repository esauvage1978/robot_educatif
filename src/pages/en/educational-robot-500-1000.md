## What this band asks of you

From 10 to 12, you still need guided sessions and a lot of blocks. From 12 to 16, several sensors, a little C or Arduino, or Python, hold up when the project lasts. From 16, or for an adult, you can aim at embedded Linux or a logic closer to engineering, provided the real price follows. Age of start also lines up with [when to start coding with kids](/en/when-to-start-coding-with-kids/).

| Type | What it trains |
|------|----------------|
| **Heavy construction** | Mechanics, stiffness, designing a model |
| **Advanced programming** | Arduino, Python, algorithms on sensors |
| **Vision** | A camera or a sensor that decides, then a motor that acts |

A demanding hobby looks for the pleasure of making something work. Preparing for further study looks for patience and for reading documentation. A maker use looks for a tool for prototypes, not only the end of the manual.

### DFRobot and HuskyLens

![DFRobot HuskyLens, an AI vision sensor for Arduino and micro:bit](/images/blog/robots-educatif-500-1000-euros-2026/dfrobot-huskylens-ai-vision.jpg)
