**mBot** and **Codey Rocky** share the [Makeblock](/en/makeblock-brand/) ecosystem and the **mBlock** software, but they suit different children. The mBot is a classic wheeled **kit** you assemble; Codey Rocky is a **head + base** duo built around interaction, a small screen and a character.

## 1. Comparison table

| | **mBot** | **Codey Rocky** |
|---|---|---|
| Form | two-motor chassis, kit to assemble | expressive head (Codey) + mobile base (Rocky) |
| Age often quoted | **8+** (screws, instructions) | **6+** (little mechanics) |
| First activities | line, obstacle, LEDs, ultrasonic | emotions, screen, mini-games on the face |
| Blocks / Python | yes, through mBlock | yes, through mBlock |
| In class | hands-on robotics workshop | companion, demos, storytelling |

**Our pick:** **Codey Rocky** for a 6–8-year-old who wants to start quickly; the **mBot** from 8 for a child who enjoys building and wants a "real robot" workshop.

## 2. Choose the mBot when…

- you want a **kit** like the ones used in clubs and schools, with screws and classic sensors;
- the goal is explicitly **STEM** with assembly and well-documented activities.

## 3. Choose Codey Rocky when…

- the child responds to a **character**, on-screen **emotions** and a fast start with little mechanics;
- you want a strong **demo** or a short project without days of assembly.

## 4. Shared limits

- **Bluetooth and updates**: plan for an adult during the first connections. See our [mBlock Bluetooth troubleshooting](/en/mblock-bluetooth-troubleshooting/) guide.
- **Several apps and modes** depending on the version: read the documentation for the exact model you buy.

## 5. Do you have to choose?

Not always: some schools use an mBot for the mechanics and terrain side and a Codey Rocky to motivate younger pupils or for storytelling projects. With a single budget, decide on **age**, appetite for **assembly** and the kind of **result** you want (technical workshop vs expressive demo).
