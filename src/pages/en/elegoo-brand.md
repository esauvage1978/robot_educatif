<p>ELEGOO’s <strong>Smart Robot Car Kit V4.0</strong> is the Arduino kit in our English guides. We haven’t had one in our hands yet, so our view draws on ELEGOO’s spec sheet and our comparisons.</p>
<h2 id="our-guides">Our guides featuring ELEGOO</h2>
<ul>
<li><a href="/en/arduino-robot-kit-for-kids/">Arduino robot kit for kids</a>: ELEGOO rates the Robot Car V4.0 13+.</li>
<li><a href="/en/best-programmable-robot-for-kids/">Best programmable robot for kids</a>: ELEGOO Robot Car V4.0 vs mBot and mBot2.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="strengths">Strengths</h2>
<ul>
<li>Complete kit with an UNO R3 board and detailed tutorials.</li>
<li>Wi-Fi control and FPV camera on the V4.0.</li>
<li>Standard Arduino parts: easy to repair and extend.</li>
<li>Low price compared with LEGO education kits.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="limits">Limits</h2>
<ul>
<li>C/C++ text coding: best from about 12–13, with adult help at first.</li>
<li>Steep learning curve: IDE, wiring, compile errors.</li>
<li>Screw assembly; less rugged than LEGO bricks.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="warranty">Warranty and after-sales service</h2>
<p>ELEGOO official policy (checked 23 October 2026): 12 months on Arduino, robot and component kits from receipt of the order; proof of purchase required; misuse, third-party repair and modification excluded. Where local law gives a longer period, ELEGOO says the longer period applies. Contact: info@elegoo.com. Source: <a href="https://www.elegoo.com/pages/warranty-period" target="_blank" rel="noopener">ELEGOO warranty periods</a>.</p>
<h2 id="verdict">Our verdict</h2>
<aside class="article-callout" role="note">
<p><strong>In short</strong></p>
<p>ELEGOO is the best-value way to learn Arduino from age 12–13. Younger children should start with a block-based robot such as the mBot.</p>
</aside>
