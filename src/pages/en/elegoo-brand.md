## Strengths

- Complete kit with an **UNO R3** board, motors, sensors and step-by-step tutorials (PDF and video on ELEGOO’s site).  
- **Wi-Fi control** and **FPV camera** on the V4.0 — unusual at this price.  
- Standard Arduino ecosystem: easy to find spare parts, shields and community projects.  
- Strong **value** compared with LEGO Education or all-in-one “premium” coding robots.

## Limits

- **C/C++ text coding** in the Arduino IDE: realistic from about **12–13**, with an adult for wiring, drivers and compile errors at first.  
- Steeper learning curve than Scratch-style robots: IDE setup, serial ports, soldering-free but fiddly screw assembly.  
- Less rugged than LEGO bricks; treat it as a **project bench** robot, not a toy to throw in a bag.

## Warranty and after-sales service

ELEGOO official policy (checked 2 November 2026): **12 months** on Arduino boards, robot kits and component kits from receipt of the order; proof of purchase required; misuse, unauthorised repair and modification excluded. Where local law gives a longer period, ELEGOO states the longer period applies. Contact **info@elegoo.com**. Source: [ELEGOO warranty periods](https://www.elegoo.com/pages/warranty-period).
