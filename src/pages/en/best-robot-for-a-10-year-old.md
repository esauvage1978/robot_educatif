**At 10, buy a robot that starts in blocks and can move to Python, if you want one purchase to last.** In England this is often Year 5; in the US, 5th grade. If the child is happy in Scratch-style blocks and the budget is tighter, the original mBot is still the right robot. If they mostly want to build three different machines, look at mBot Ranger. An Arduino car is a different hobby: the maker of the ELEGOO Smart Robot Car V4.0 rates it 13+, so at 10 it is an adult-led project, not a toy they wire alone.

Plan about an hour of your time before the first session to [install mBlock 5](/en/install-mblock-5-windows/) and check the connection. The child should not meet a driver error as their first coding experience.

Figures below for sensors and battery come from Makeblock's and ELEGOO's product pages, as checked for our comparison on 21 October 2026. They can change; the official page wins if a listing disagrees.

## What changes around 10

They can debug a program ("the robot turns too soon, so this block is wrong") and keep a project over more than one sitting. They still do not need C++ or Linux. Text coding is a door you open, not the entry ticket.

## A simple decision

Budget allows and they will keep the robot for years: mBot2. Stay on blocks: mBot. They love mechanics more than code: Ranger. They want electronics with you, and you accept the 13+ rating: ELEGOO, after reading the Arduino guide.

## Questions parents ask

### Is Python required at 10?

No. Blocks are enough. Python is the reason to prefer mBot2 if you want the same robot in two years.

### Can they assemble mBot2 alone?

Plan to do the first assembly together. Half an hour with the manual is normal. The child can lead the coding after that.

## Frequently asked questions

### Best robot for a 10-year-old?
Prefer a robot that can grow: **mBot2** for blocks and Python, or **mBot** if you stay mainly in blocks.

### Need mBlock before Christmas?
Yes — budget about an hour to [install mBlock 5](/en/install-mblock-5-windows/) so the first session is not a driver fight.

### mBot or mBot2 at 10?
**mBot** for a simpler, cheaper start; **mBot2** for Python and a longer runway — see [mBot vs mBot2](/en/mbot-vs-mbot2/).
