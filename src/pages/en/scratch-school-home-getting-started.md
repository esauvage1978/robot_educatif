> **Direct answer (October 2026)** — To start **Scratch** at home or at school: create an account on [scratch.mit.edu](https://scratch.mit.edu) (free), set a **weekly 30–45 min slot**, and follow **one finishable project** (e.g. [the square](/activite-scratch-le-carre/)). Autonomy on blocks usually appears at **8–11** with an adult for saving; from **7**, short guided activities work well.

**Scratch** (and Scratch-like tools such as **mBlock** for Makeblock robots) remains an excellent **first language** in 2026: visual, widely used in textbooks, and easy to share.
