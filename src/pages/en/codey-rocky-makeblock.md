**Codey Rocky** is an educational robot from **[Makeblock](/en/makeblock-brand/)** in two parts: a programmable **“head” module (Codey)** with a screen and sensors, and a **mobile base (Rocky)** so it can move. It is often aimed at **ages 6 and up** and fits into **mBlock 5**, like the [mBot2](/en/mbot2-makeblock-what-it-teaches/).

## Teaching strengths

![Block programming workshop in the style of mBlock / Scratch (mood illustration)](/images/blog/codey-rocky-mblock-atelier.webp)

*A “visual code” block: the same logic you use in mBlock applies to compatible Makeblock robots.*

- **Ready to use**: little or no complex mechanical assembly. The focus is **programming** and **interaction**.
- **Blocks, then text**: you can progress from **Scratch (blocks)** to **Python**, through the apps and the **mBlock** IDE — see [Install mBlock 5 on Windows 10](/en/install-mblock-5-windows/) and [First steps with mBlock 5](/en/first-steps-mblock-5/).
- **Several sensors**: a rich environment for **logic, events, and sensors**, without staying on a simple “remote control.” As a complement, the activities in [Scratch](/en/scratch-video-game-scrolling-backdrop/) or [on an mBot](/en/first-mbot-program/) show the same block logic.
- **Brick compatible**: you can **customize** the base with Lego Technic-style or compatible builds (check the manufacturer’s documentation).

### A bank of commented examples (script ideas)

The site [**Pakronics Learn — Examples**](https://learn.pakronics.com.au/primary/codey-rocky-ai-robot/examples) describes **seven typical mini-projects** in mBlock, very close to what you do in class:

1. **Emotions and buttons** — chain sounds and “faces” on the screen from a button.
2. **LED / image animations** — chain displays with **motion** or shake detection.
3. **Musician** — play notes, control the volume with the dial.
4. **Blink** — loops and **randomness** for a realistic blink.
5. **Color** — react to a detected color (sounds + LED, depending on the scenario).
6. **Avoid an obstacle** — distance sensor + movement commands on Rocky.
7. **Scratch sprites + robot** — link the **stage** (sprites) and the robot’s actions.

For **sending the program to the robot** (it then runs on its own), see also their page [**Upload mode**](https://learn.pakronics.com.au/primary/codey-rocky-ai-robot/tutorials/upload-mode).

## Limits to plan for

- **Software ecosystem**: several apps (remote control, programming, and so on) — allow a little time for the **tutorial and the first connections** (Bluetooth, tablet or PC).
- **Support**: for a **6–8-year-old**, an adult often helps with **updates**, Bluetooth **pairing**, and the first **uploads**.
- Compared with a classic “tank” **mBot**, the positioning is more **companion / expressive screen** than **a kit you screw together from start to finish**.

## Activity ideas

1. Show **emotions** or short messages on the robot’s “face.”
2. **Avoid an obstacle**, or react to a distance with the matching sensors.
3. Chain a **light sequence and a movement** to understand **event blocks**.
