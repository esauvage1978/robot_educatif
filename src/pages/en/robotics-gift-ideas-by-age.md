Christmas night, the first week of term and a birthday do not ask for the same present. A box that takes too long to open on the 24th ends in frustration. At the start of term the robot is used only if it is already set up and within reach. For a birthday the age is precise: a taste (a vehicle, building, with or without a screen) beats a shop label that says “8–12”.

The figures below are an Amazon.fr reading on 6 November 2026 at 11:23, in euros. They are not a UK or US price, and this English page does not link those listings as if the ASIN were the same product abroad. An accessory that was not on that reading is not given a number.

## What makes the gift get used

| Factor | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Adult time | Who helps on the first day? |
| Space | Floor, table, or a corner of a desk? |
| How long it lasts | Batteries, parts, a community |
| Ritual | An hour together beats a gadget opened alone |

The usual block is software that is out of date, or the wrong cable. A note in the card (which computer or tablet, Wi-Fi if the model needs it, an mBlock account) avoids the next morning. Age bands: [how to choose by age](/en/best-programmable-robot-for-kids/).

## About 5 to 7

Play is on the floor, with short instructions. A path without a screen (cards, buttons, a robot that goes from one point to another) lines instructions up without an hour of tutorial on the day. See [a robot for a 5-year-old](/en/best-robot-for-a-5-year-old/).

On the French shop Cultura, the French page of this guide names two Clementoni robots, Eddie and Mon Robot 2.0. The recommended age is on those listings. Their price is not in the Amazon.fr reading.

## About 8 to 10

Sessions get longer: Scratch, a small build, sometimes a first sensor. The same mBot, at **89.99 euros** on that reading, fits if an adult can spend an afternoon on the first connection and which way the motors turn. The age page is [a robot for an 8-year-old](/en/best-robot-for-an-8-year-old/).

The French Cultura listings also name the Buki Robot Marko, a kit to assemble that is not programmed, and the Pandacraft “Les robots” kit. Their prices are not in this reading.

## Around the robot, with no fake total

The kit stops if the batteries are empty or a screw has rolled under the sofa. Plan the cell size written on the notice (AA, AAA or a dedicated pack), a box for screws and sensors, and a spare cable of the right plug (micro-USB or USB-C, depending on the generation). None of those is priced on this page. Read each price, and do not add them to the robot to print a “gift budget” that was never read as a total.

A card in the parcel that offers one hour of setup together is the part that keeps the robot out of the cupboard.
