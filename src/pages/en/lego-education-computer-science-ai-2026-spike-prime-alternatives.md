Since **30 June 2026**, **LEGO Education** no longer sells **SPIKE Prime/Essential** direct; the announced successor is the **Computer Science & AI** kit (**from April 2026**, **$339.95** US MSRP). It bundles a programmable hub, simplified **AI/vision** sensors, and **Python** pathways.

## Positioning

- **Audience**: middle–high school CS standards.
- **Price**: about **$340**—roughly **€170 more** than a standalone **mBot2**.
- **Trade-off**: LEGO parts get lost; software is less open than **mBlock** + Arduino paths.

## Alternatives we document

1. **[mBot2](/en/makeblock-brand/)** (~€168)—Python, Wi-Fi, no studs.
2. **micro:bit V2** plus chassis—lower budget.
