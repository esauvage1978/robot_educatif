Since **30 June 2026**, **LEGO Education** no longer sells **SPIKE Prime/Essential** direct; the announced successor is the **Computer Science & AI** kit (**from April 2026**, **around $340** US MSRP). It bundles a programmable hub, simplified **AI/vision** sensors, and **Python** pathways aimed at middle–high school computer-science standards.

For background on SPIKE, WeDo and reseller reality, read our [LEGO Education brand guide](/en/lego-education-brand/). For brick-vs-robot workshops, [mBot vs LEGO robot](/en/mbot-vs-lego-robot/) stays the main comparison.

<h2 id="context">SPIKE exit and the CS &amp; AI kit</h2>

LEGO’s **SPIKE update 2026** page confirms direct catalogue end for SPIKE sets while the **SPIKE app** stays supported until **2031** and **SPIKE Prime** remains eligible in **FIRST LEGO League** through **2027-2028**. Families hunting **new** LEGO hub kits therefore face **reseller prices** for legacy SPIKE or the **CS &amp; AI** box at ~**$340**.

**Trade-offs vs open robotics:** Technic builds motivate brick lovers; parts scatter at home. Software is less open than **mBlock** + **Arduino** paths on Makeblock or ELEGOO. AI features depend on LEGO’s app roadmap — verify lesson fit before a class purchase.
