<p>LEGO Education SPIKE is the benchmark we compare most educational robots against. Our detailed comparisons are in French; we have not tested SPIKE at home.</p>
<aside class="article-callout" role="note">
<p><strong>Important: SPIKE is being withdrawn</strong></p>
<p>LEGO Education has announced the end of sale of the SPIKE portfolio (Essential and Prime): no direct sales after 30 June 2026, retailers sell remaining stock. The SPIKE app is supported until 30 June 2031. Source: <a href="https://education.lego.com/en-us/spike-update-2026/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">LEGO Education</a>.</p>
</aside>

<h2 id="strengths">Strengths</h2>
<ul>
<li>Building: one hub, dozens of official models and inventions.</li>
<li>Very guided interface; bricks easier than screws for under-8s.</li>
<li>Excellent robustness.</li>
<li>Closely aligned with FIRST LEGO League.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="limits">Limits</h2>
<ul>
<li>High price, especially for a classroom.</li>
<li>Closed ecosystem: LEGO extensions only.</li>
<li>Longer build before coding.</li>
<li>Now being withdrawn from sale.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="support">Support and warranty</h2>
<p>LEGO Education support is online at <a href="https://education.lego.com/en-us/support/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">education.lego.com/support</a>. Your statutory rights against the retailer still apply.</p>
<h2 id="verdict">Our verdict</h2>
<aside class="article-callout" role="note">
<p><strong>In short</strong></p>
<p>LEGO remains the best choice for brick-loving children, schools already equipped and FIRST LEGO League teams. For a new purchase, factor in the end of sale; on a budget or aiming for Python, see <a href="/en/makeblock-brand/">Makeblock</a>.</p>
