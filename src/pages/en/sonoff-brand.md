<h2 id="strengths">Strengths</h2>
<ul>
<li>ZBDongle-E: EFR32MG21 chip, external antenna, works with ZHA and Zigbee2MQTT, well documented.</li>
<li>SNZB-02D: LCD screen, ±0.2 °C, ~2-year battery (maker figure; room temperature affects life).</li>
<li>Large catalogue of mains-powered Zigbee plugs that strengthen the mesh.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="limits">Limits</h2>
<ul>
<li>No Thread/Matter by default on the ZBDongle-E (Zigbee firmware).</li>
<li>Dongle Lite: internal antenna, shorter range in noisy 2.4 GHz homes.</li>
<li>Beware of counterfeit “SONOFF” copies — buy from known sellers.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="warranty">Warranty</h2>
<p>SONOFF/ITEAD (checked 23 October 2026): 2-year limited warranty from purchase for quality defects, via the ITEAD support centre with proof of purchase; flashing or modifying firmware voids it. Source: <a href="https://sonoff.tech/en-us/pages/return-refund" target="_blank" rel="noopener">SONOFF warranty</a>.</p>
