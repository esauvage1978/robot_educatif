<p>SONOFF (an ITEAD brand) features in our Zigbee guides for Home Assistant.</p>
<h2 id="our-guides">Our guides featuring SONOFF</h2>
<ul>
<li><a href="/en/smart-home/best-zigbee-dongle-home-assistant/">Best Zigbee dongle for Home Assistant</a>: ZBDongle-E for most people, Dongle Lite MG21 for the smallest budget.</li>
<li><a hreflang="fr" href="/domotique/capteur-temperature-zigbee/">Zigbee temperature sensors (in French)</a>: SNZB-02D is our pick.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="strengths">Strengths</h2>
<ul>
<li>ZBDongle-E: EFR32MG21 chip, external antenna, works with ZHA and Zigbee2MQTT, well documented.</li>
<li>SNZB-02D: LCD screen, ±0.2 °C, 2-year battery life.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="limits">Limits</h2>
<ul>
<li>No Thread/Matter by default on the ZBDongle-E.</li>
<li>Dongle Lite: internal antenna, shorter range.</li>
<li>Beware of counterfeit “SONOFF” copies.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="warranty">Warranty</h2>
<p>SONOFF/ITEAD (checked 23 October 2026): 2-year limited warranty from purchase for quality defects, via the ITEAD support centre with proof of purchase; flashing or modifying firmware voids it. Source: <a href="https://sonoff.tech/en-us/pages/return-refund" target="_blank" rel="noopener">SONOFF warranty</a>.</p>
<h2 id="verdict">Our verdict</h2>
<aside class="article-callout" role="note">
<p><strong>In short</strong></p>
<p>The SONOFF ZBDongle-E is our first choice to start a Zigbee network on Home Assistant.</p>
