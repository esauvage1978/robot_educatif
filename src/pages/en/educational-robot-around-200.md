## How to read this band

From 6 to 8, visual programming holds up better: large blocks, little text, sometimes color codes on paper before any screen. From 8 to 12, conditions, loops and sensors become the game, as long as the robot answers at once (avoid an obstacle, follow a line). From 12, many families move to Arduino, Python or a longer build: the [Raspberry Pi or robot kit guide](/en/raspberry-pi-or-robot-kit-for-teens/) compares those paths.

The kind of learning changes the right box. A playful kit has to succeed quickly, without jargon. A technical kit also teaches mechanics and electronics, at the cost of assembly. A kit designed for school brings worksheets and, sometimes, resources for a club. Level follows: a fast unboxing suits a first contact; several hours of building is more like 9 or 10, with an adult at the start.
